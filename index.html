Classic Pac-Man

Twelve levels. Three lives per play. When you beat a high score, you'll be asked for a name. Top 10 is saved server-side when available.

How to play
  • Move: Use the Arrow keys (Up, Down, Left, Right) to steer Pac-Man.
  • Start / Pause: Press the Space bar to start the game or pause and resume during play.
  • Objective: Eat all the dots to clear the level. Power pellets let you eat frightened ghosts for bonus points.
  • Lives: You get 3 lives per play. Losing all lives ends the game.
  • High scores: When you hit a top-10 score, you'll be prompted for a name. Use the High Scores button to view the leaderboard. If the server is unreachable, scores are saved locally until the server is available again.
  • Difficulty rises across 12 levels: ghosts speed up and Pac-Man gets faster.
  • Pac-Man's mouth faces the direction you move him.
  • If your browser lacks required features, you'll see a short compatibility message.
